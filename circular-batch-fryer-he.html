Circular Batch Fryer with Pellet Heat Exchanger

Precision-engineered circular batch fryer integrated with an external pellet-fired multi-pass heat exchanger, continuous circulation pump, and stainless steel oil holding vessel.

The Kravionex Circular Batch Fryer with External Pellet Heat Exchanger is designed for snack manufacturers demanding the highest oil life, exact temperature stability, and lowest possible operating costs. Unlike direct-fired pans that cause hot spots and scorched oil, thermal transfer occurs outside the frying kettle in an insulated multi-pass heat exchanger. Frying oil is continuously drawn from the pan, gently elevated in temperature across low-delta-T heating tubes, and recirculated with laminar flow across the frying bed.

🍟 Potato Chips 🍌 Banana Wafers 🟡 Crisp Boondi 🥜 Masala Peanuts ✨ 2D/3D Pellets

Key Features

External Heat Exchanger
