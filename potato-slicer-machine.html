High-Speed Commercial Potato and Plantain Slicer

High-speed vegetable slicing system featuring water injection feed, rotary cutting rotor with hardened stainless steel blades, and mobile base for potato chips lines.

The Kravionex High-Speed Commercial Slicer uses centrifugal rotor geometry that feeds tubers against precision-honed stainless steel cutting knives. Water injection flumes immediately rinse surface starch away, ensuring slices separate cleanly in the fryer and cook evenly.
